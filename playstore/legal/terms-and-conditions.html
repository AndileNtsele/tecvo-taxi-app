02
What the App Does

Taxi combines real-time map visibility with community features for the South African minibus taxi industry.

The app provides
  • A live map showing taxis and passengers going the same direction
  • TOWN and LOCAL direction selection
  • Automatic removal of your location when you leave the map
  • Community Chat — city-based public chat rooms for the taxi community
  • A chat profile — display name and optional profile picture used in Community Chat
The app does not provide
  • Ride booking or reservation of any kind
  • Payment processing between drivers and passengers
  • Guaranteed taxi availability or arrival times
  • Dispatch instructions or route management
