4. PRIVACY AND DATA COLLECTION

4.1 Real-Time Visibility Service

The App operates as a real-time visibility service:

  • Location data is processed while using the map screen
  • Location tracking continues when you switch apps (calls, WhatsApp, etc.) - keeps you visible while waiting
  • All location data is automatically deleted when you close the map or exit the app
  • No permanent location history or trip records are stored

4.2 Background Location Usage

The App:

  • Continues tracking location when you use other apps while waiting for taxi
  • Shows persistent notification ('Taxi - Active') while tracking in background
  • Stops tracking completely when you close the map screen or exit the app
  • Uses background location to maintain your visibility to drivers/passengers

4.3 Service-Based Model
