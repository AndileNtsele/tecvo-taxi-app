Taxi
Privacy Policy
Last updated: January 2026  ·  Version 1.0

The Taxi app is a real-time visibility service for South African commuters and taxi drivers — not a data collection platform. Your location is processed temporarily while you use the service and removed automatically the moment you leave the map. No history. No profiles. No tracking.

01
What This App Is

Taxi (by TECVO) gives drivers and passengers a real-time birds-eye view of who is at taxi pickup points and where they are going. It enhances the existing taxi experience — you still walk to your usual spot, you just now have visibility of what is coming.

Like Google Maps showing your location during navigation, or WhatsApp's live location feature, Taxi processes your location only while the service is actively in use. The moment the service ends, the data is gone.

03
How the Service Works
01
You walk to your usual taxi pickup point — nothing about your routine changes.
02
You open the app, select your role (Driver or Passenger) and your direction (TOWN or LOCAL).
03
The live map shows your position to other users going the same direction. Passengers see taxis. Drivers see passengers.
04
You leave the map screen. Your location is removed from the system immediately and automatically. No data remains.
04
Third-Party Services

We use the following services to deliver the app. Each is operationally necessary — we use nothing else.

Firebase (Google)

Powers real-time location visibility and phone authentication. Temporary location data is written to and removed from Firebase in real time. Google's Firebase Privacy Policy applies: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Google Maps Platform

Displays the map and processes location data for geocoding. Google's Privacy Policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy

Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics

Crashlytics captures anonymised crash data (device model, OS version, app version, crash context) to help us fix bugs. Analytics captures aggregated usage patterns to help us improve features. Neither service has access to your location data or phone number.

05
Your Rights Under POPIA

The Protection of Personal Information Act gives you the following rights in relation to your personal information:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Objection — object to processing of your information.
  • Complaint — lodge a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator at inforeg@justice.gov.za
To delete your account: Open the app → Settings → Delete Account → complete phone verification → deletion is immediate. Alternatively, email andile@tecvo.co.za with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and your registered phone number. Processed within 7 business days.
06
Security

All data transmitted by the app uses HTTPS/TLS encryption. Firebase security rules prevent any unauthorised access to the database. Because location data is automatically deleted when the service ends, the risk profile of a data breach is materially lower than apps that retain permanent user data.

07
Children

This app is designed for adults using South African taxi services. It is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly process personal information from minors. If we become aware that a minor has registered, that account will be deleted immediately.
