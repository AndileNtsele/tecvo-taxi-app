02
What the App Does

Taxi is built for the South African minibus taxi industry. It combines real-time map visibility with community features designed to connect drivers, passengers, and taxi associations.

The app provides
  • A live map showing taxis and passengers going the same direction
  • TOWN and LOCAL direction selection — the standard terminology used at SA taxi ranks
  • Automatic removal of your location when you leave the map
  • Community Chat — city-based public chat rooms for drivers, passengers, and commuters
  • Announcements — official notices posted by verified taxi associations
  • Taxi Association Registration — a way for associations to create a verified presence in the app
  • A chat profile — a display name and optional profile picture used in Community Chat
The app does not provide
  • Ride booking or reservation of any kind
  • Payment processing between drivers and passengers
  • Guaranteed taxi availability or arrival times
  • Dispatch instructions or route management
